## Movement, pauses and print candidates

- **30.** Hold on the tip and Charlie's line. Pull back to reveal the submerged labels, then replace the iceberg with the Internet row and add the blockchain row underneath. Pause to compare infrastructure and applications. Land on the three ledgers and replace the transaction rows with the same non-payment records in each. Hold and ask what agreement on a record can tell us. Print the final ledger view.
- **31.** Bring the four panels in one by one. Transition from the credential panel to the college issuing Alice's credential, then to the employer receiving it and independently checking the signature, issuer and status. Pause to ask which data the registry needs. Show the three design examples before revealing any answers and invite verbal predictions. Print the final decision view.
- **32.** Follow the transcript across the interview desk, enlarge it, and highlight the graduation row and surplus fields as Bob notices the failed attempts. Pause on Alice's question. Replace the interview with the original café and show the three matching copies. Transition to the transparent walls around Alice. Hold on the glass house and ask the privacy question aloud. Print the glass house.

## Speaker notes and sources

Disclose the authored examples. The iceberg is a conceptual image with no quantitative meaning. Agreement on records cannot establish external truth. The issuer remains trusted for credential accuracy. The employer receives the credential from Alice, while the shared registry provides issuer and status information. A signature and conventional PKI may suffice, depending on the trust model. Timestamping can provide evidence that data existed by a given time. The café copies reuse slide 9’s two IOUs without adding a balance or assigning a new coffee amount. The glass house represents disclosure and makes no claim about what is actually on-chain. [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) · [RFC 3161 time-stamp protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html)
